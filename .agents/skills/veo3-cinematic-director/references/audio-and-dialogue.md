# Audio and dialogue

Plan separate layers: principal voice, environmental bed, synchronized effects and optional music. Voice should remain intelligible; traffic and engine sounds can be present but lower in the mix during speech. Echo/reverb should match enclosure and distance.

An on-camera speaker uses synchronized mouth movement; voice-over does not require the character to move lips. Preserve exact supplied text. No background characters should utter invented words. Silence and ambient-only instructions take priority over cinematic music habits.

Duration estimate: word count divided by delivery pace plus pauses and action time. This is a planning heuristic, not measured audio. If the line cannot fit, expose the conflict instead of silently cutting or speeding speech. Suggested rewrites must be explicitly labelled and used only with permission.
