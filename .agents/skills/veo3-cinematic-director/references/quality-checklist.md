# Quality checklist

1. Intent preserved and fixed dialogue copied exactly.
2. Subject and relationships clear.
3. Setting and cultural context consistent.
4. Actions ordered with believable physics.
5. Style follows the brief.
6. Camera has useful framing/path and no contradiction.
7. Light, shadows and colour motivated.
8. Voice/ambience/effects/music priorities explicit.
9. Spoken language and speaker attribution correct.
10. Duration fits action and speech or conflict is disclosed.
11. No unsolicited cuts, dialogue, score or transformations.
12. Negative constraints do not suppress requested content.
13. Sequence anchors remain consistent.
14. Output is copyable and respects requested quantity/length.
15. No claimed rendering, hidden knowledge or unverified feature guarantee.

Structural checks are automatable. Visual fidelity, speech naturalness and render quality need real execution.
