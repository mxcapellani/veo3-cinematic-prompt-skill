# Lighting and colour

Identify motivated key source, direction, softness, ambient fill and practical accents. Shadow and reflection should agree with the source and material. Rain and glass can reveal highlights, but do not turn every night scene into neon.

Grade to support narrative while preserving material and skin plausibility. Dramatic light is a choice of direction, contrast and visibility, not simply darkness. If a reference defines light, retain it unless user permits change.

Atmosphere must fit location: daylight dust may appear in a real shaft of light; fog is not mandatory in a clean studio. Avoid invisible moving suns or inconsistent colour temperatures without physical explanation.
