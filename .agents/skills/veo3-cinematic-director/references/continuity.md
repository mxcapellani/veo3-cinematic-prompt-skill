# Continuity ledger

Record character ID/appearance, wardrobe, props, location, time/light, screen direction, emotional state, initial state and final state for each shot. Repeat essential anchors in independent prompts.

Example ledger: Person A wears an olive jacket and carries a closed notebook in the left hand; enters a cafe from left to right; the next shot starts inside after crossing the door. Person B remains seated by the window until the meeting action. Do not have A already sitting in the next shot without an arrival or cut establishing it.

Alternative variations are not a sequence. Independent generations do not automatically preserve identity. Reference controls may help where supported, but must not be presented as guaranteed or universally available.
