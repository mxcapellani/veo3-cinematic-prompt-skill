# Camera and lenses

24mm equivalent can support environmental scale; 35mm a human-in-context perspective; 50mm a balanced portrait/scene; 85mm a tighter portrait look. Camera distance controls perspective. Focal length language communicates intended appearance, not hardware settings enforced by every generator.

Tracking follows subject movement; dolly moves camera through space; zoom changes apparent field of view. Pan/tilt rotate direction; crane changes elevated spatial view; orbit needs a clear path and stable subject anchor. Handheld describes support behaviour, not arbitrary shaking.

Choose one dominant trajectory with start, path, speed and end. Static then dolly is sequential, not simultaneous. Keep framing compatible with acting: a lip-sync close-up needs mouth visibility, while a wide shot cannot guarantee subtle facial detail. Do not cut when a single continuous shot is requested.
