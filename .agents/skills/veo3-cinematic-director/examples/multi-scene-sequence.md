# Three-scene sequence

Input: Crie três cenas consecutivas de uma pessoa entrando em um café, encontrando alguém e iniciando uma conversa.

Illustrative outputs only. The supplied idea contains no spoken words, so these prompts stage the start of conversation without inventing dialogue.

## Scene 1

```text
SCENE OVERVIEW
A person enters a neighborhood cafe.

SUBJECT AND CHARACTER DETAILS
Person A: olive jacket, dark trousers, closed notebook initially in the left hand. Person B: beige sweater, seated near the window. Keep their appearances consistent across scenes.

ENVIRONMENT AND SETTING
The same neighborhood cafe, entrance on the left and window table deeper to the right, daylight and modest background furnishings.

ACTION AND PERFORMANCE
Person A in an olive jacket pushes open the cafe door while holding a closed notebook in the left hand, then steps inside. Person B in a beige sweater remains seated by the window.

CINEMATOGRAPHY AND CAMERA DIRECTION
A medium-wide view from inside follows the doorway entry with a restrained pan.

LIGHTING AND COLOR GRADING
Consistent soft window daylight from the same side and restrained warm interior tones.

AUDIO AND SOUND DESIGN
Low cafe room tone, appropriate footsteps and small furniture/prop sounds. No intelligible background dialogue or music.

TIMING AND CONTINUITY
Start outside the open doorway; finish just inside with A looking toward the window table.

NEGATIVE CONSTRAINTS
No subtitles, text overlays, watermarks or unintended glitch effects.

FINAL GENERATION INSTRUCTIONS
Maintain coherent physics, stable appearance and readable action. Follow the described shot rather than adding an unsolicited montage.
```
